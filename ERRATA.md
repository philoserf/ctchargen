# ERRATA

Interpretations of and deviations from the held text (Books 1–3, © 1977
text, FFE reprints), each with its page cite. Nothing here is applied
silently: every record stamps, in its `errata` array, the identifiers of
the entries that governed its generation. Page cites are printed page
numbers (PDF page N+6 in Book 1).

## E001 — The draft is optional: "may" governs over "must" (p. 5)

P. 5 prints both readings: the Enlistment paragraph's "If rejected for
enlistment, he may submit to the draft" and the Draft paragraph's opening
"Should an attempt at enlistment fail, the character must submit to the
draft." The **may** reading governs here. Declining the draft ends
generation with an 18-year-old civilian — a valid, if bare, record.
Stamped on every record: the choice point exists for every rejected
character.

## E002 — Per-term order is the exposition's (pp. 5–6 vs p. 24)

The exposition presents each term as survival, commission, promotion,
skills, then reenlistment (pp. 5–6), but the Jamison example rolls
reenlistment before its term's skills (p. 24). The exposition's order
governs. Replay makes the order load-bearing: it fixes dice-stream
consumption, so changing it is an engine version bump. Stamped on every
record.

## E003 — Age of a character killed in service (p. 5)

Survival failure is death (p. 5); the book does not state the dead
character's age. The record keeps the age at the start of the fatal term
and records the term and cause (FR8). Stamped on records with a death.

## Normalizations (not stamped)

Spelling is normalized to the skills list's own names, so one skill
accumulates under one name:

- "Electronics" (Other, Advanced Education 8+ table row 3, p. 11) is
  recorded as **Electronic** (the tables' and the Jamison example's
  spelling, pp. 11, 25).
- "Jack-o-T" (p. 11) is recorded as **Jack-of-all-Trades** (p. 20).
